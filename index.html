Technology. Accessibility. Thinking differently.

Accessibility isn't about doing less. It's about finding another way.

I'm Jeff. I build iPhone apps for problems I run into myself, then I share them so you don't have to squint, guess, or give up either. Here's what I've made so far.

iPhone app

Lupek

Zoom in. Ask. Understand.

A magnifier that answers questions. Point your camera at a receipt, a label, or the fine print on a package, select the part you care about, and ask. Lupek reads it and tells you what it means.

  • 1Select just the part you need. Drag around one line instead of fighting the whole page.
  • 2Ask in plain words. "What kind of sandpaper did I buy?" works.
  • 3Keep going. Ask a follow-up, have the answer read aloud, or copy it.

iPhone game

GlyphMatch

VVVOX

Match three. No colors required.

I'm color blind, so I built a match three game I could actually play. Every tile is a letter, so nothing depends on telling one color from another. Line up three of the same letter, clear the board, and chase three stars on every level.
